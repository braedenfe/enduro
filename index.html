Why enduro exists

Most activewear is plastic. Petroleum spun into thread, worn against your skin, shed into the ocean with every wash. We think the gear you sweat in should come from the earth, not the refinery. So we build with merino, organic cotton and nothing synthetic. Nothing.

01 · Merino

Grown, not made.

17.5 micron mulesing free merino. Temperature regulating, odour resistant after back to back sessions, soft enough to forget you are wearing it.

02 · Organic cotton

Soft on skin, easy on earth.

Organic ring spun combed cotton blended with modal. Panel washed for a lived in feel from the first wear, certified OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

03 · Zero synthetics

No plastic. Ever.

No polyester, no nylon, no elastane hiding in the seams. Every garment returns to the earth the way it came from it.
