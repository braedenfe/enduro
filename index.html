Why enduro exists

Most performance wear is built from synthetic fibres. We chose a different path. Natural materials that breathe easier, feel better against the skin, and are designed for movement from the beginning. Merino wool. Organic cotton. Nothing unnecessary. Built for the long run.

01 · Merino

Grown, not made.

17.5 micron mulesing free merino. Temperature regulating, odour resistant after back to back sessions, soft enough to forget you are wearing it.

02 · Organic cotton

Soft on skin, easy on earth.

100% organic cotton — breathable, lightweight, and incredibly soft from the first wear. Certified to GOTS, OCS, and OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

03 · Microplastics

Plastic doesn’t just wash away.

Synthetic fabrics shed microplastics through wear and washing. Those fibres are now found in rivers, oceans and even us. Researchers are exploring whether exercise, with its heat, friction and harder breathing, may draw more in through breath and skin. It’s why we build around natural fibres.

Extra fine merino

Merino is graded by fibre diameter in microns. At 17.5 microns, ours sits below the human itch threshold of around 21, so it feels soft worn directly against skin, run after run.
