Why enduro exists

Most performance wear is built from synthetic fibres. We chose a different path. Natural materials that breathe easier, feel better against the skin, and are designed for movement from the beginning. Merino wool. Organic cotton. Nothing unnecessary. Built for the long run.

01 · Merino

Grown, not made.

17.5 micron mulesing-free Woolmark certified merino wool. Itch-free, temperature regulating (cool when it’s hot, warm when it’s cold), and naturally odour resistant so you can wash less. Built for running, the gym and travel. Soft enough to forget you’re wearing it.

02 · Organic cotton

Soft on skin, easy on earth.

100% organic cotton. Breathable, lightweight and soft from the first wear, and softer with every wear. Gentle on skin, tested for harmful substances and certified to GOTS, OCS and OEKO-TEX Standard 100. Yet durable for everyday wear, training and travel.

03 · Microplastics

Plastic doesn’t just wash away.

Synthetic fabrics shed microplastics every time you move and sweat. During exercise, heat, friction and deeper breathing can drive those fibres, and the chemicals they carry, into your lungs and through your skin. That’s why we build only with natural fibres.
