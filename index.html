Why We Exist

Peak performance was never engineered in a lab. It was refined by nature.

Most performance apparel is petroleum-based plastic. It traps heat, sheds microplastics, and relies on chemical finishes to function.

We build with fibres that have been performing for millennia. Every enduro piece is made entirely from natural materials selected for comfort, durability and how they behave when you move.

i
Nothing synthetic to shed +
Synthetic garments release plastic fibres during wear and washing. Natural fibres contain no plastic, so there is nothing synthetic to leave behind, on your skin or in the water.
ii
Skin that can breathe +
Natural fibres regulate temperature and humidity at the skin, reducing the hot, clammy feel of synthetics. Comfort that holds from the first kilometre to the last.
iii
Naturally odour resistant +
Merino manages moisture and limits the conditions bacteria need to thrive. Freshness without silver treatments or chemical finishes, run after run.
iv
No unnecessary chemistry +
Many synthetic fabrics depend on coatings and additives to wick and perform. Natural fibres move moisture through their own structure, so the fabric stays simple and close to the source.

The fibres.

Selected, not synthesised

Merino Wool

Ultra-fine merino, knitted for performance. Temperature regulating, naturally odour resistant, and softer against the skin than any synthetic. Performs from dawn trails to the commute home.

17 to 18.5µ Fineness Mulesing free Sourcing

Organic Cotton

Ring spun, combed organic cotton blended with modal for drape and recovery. Exceptionally soft, breathable and durable. The foundation of the Long Run Tee.

155gsm Weight OEKO-TEX 100 Certified

Tencel™

Lyocell from sustainably sourced eucalyptus, produced in a closed-loop process that recycles water and solvents. Smooth, cool and naturally moisture managing.

Closed loop Process Biodegradable End of life

Hemp & Bamboo

In development for future pieces. Hemp is the strongest natural fibre on earth and only improves with wear. Bamboo is silky, antibacterial and remarkably renewable.
