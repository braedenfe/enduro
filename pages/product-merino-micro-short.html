Can you wear merino wool in the sauna?

Yes. MerinoFlow™ is designed to be comfortable from 20 to 80°C, so it moves from the studio floor to the sauna. Merino breathes, manages moisture and resists odour naturally, which is why we build our studio pieces around it.

What is the gusset made of?

The double-layer gusset is lined in 100% merino, so only natural fibre sits against your most delicate skin. The lining also adds a discreet smoothing layer through the front.

Are the micro shorts seamless?

They are not seamless. They are finished with flatlock seams, which lie flat against the skin for a smooth, supportive fit with no chafe points.

What size should I choose?

Sizes run XS (AU 6 to 8) to L (AU 12 to 14), with a mid-rise waistband for gentle, all-day support. If you are between sizes, choose down for a more secure fit or up for a more relaxed fit. Our model wears a size S.

Why is it 95% merino and 5% spandex?

Merino leads the fabric for breathability, temperature regulation and softness. A small 5% spandex content gives the knit the stretch and recovery it needs to hold its shape through movement.

Is there a matching set?

Yes. Pair them with the MerinoFlow™ Bandeau and 20% comes off both pieces, applied automatically in your cart.

Where are they made?

Designed and made in Melbourne, Australia, from Australian merino wool.

How do I wash merino activewear?

Cold machine wash with a Woolmark-approved detergent. Do not bleach or soak, and do not tumble dry. Cool iron if needed, avoiding the print.

What you’ll noticeWhy natural fibres

Drier, not damp. Polyester can’t absorb sweat, so it sits on your skin and sticks to you almost straight away. Merino and cotton draw moisture in and let it breathe out, so you stay drier for longer.

Breathes with you. Merino and cotton let air and heat move through the fabric instead of trapping them against your body like plastic. Merino also helps regulate your temperature as you warm up and cool down.

Fresh for longer. Polyester holds onto odour, even after washing. Merino and cotton don’t cling to it the same way, and merino naturally resists odour, so you can wear more and wash less.

Soft against your skin. Ultra fine merino with no itch, and cotton that gets softer with every wash. No plastic sheen, no static, nothing sticking to you.
